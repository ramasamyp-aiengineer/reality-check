Why today's answers fail

Search it yourself

20+ minutes of tabs. You assemble the sources and still don't check the RBI list.

Ask an AI chatbot

Answers from memory. No sources, and new apps are unknown to the model.

Fact-check sites

Reliable but slow, and they only cover claims that already went viral.

The insight: the ground truth already exists, on Google and in regulator lists. What is missing is something that checks all of it, in seconds, the moment a forward arrives.

The idea

Small agents that each prove one thing with live Google evidence, composed like Lego, judged by rules.

01 Paste

A forward, link, stock tip, image or business profile.

02 Plan

The planner wires a validated graph of agents. Edit it on the canvas.

03 Approve

See the exact SerpApi cost per engine. Nothing is spent without approval.

04 Run

Agents run in parallel. Searches and evidence stream in live.

05 Verdict

Rules decide. Status, decision, confidence and every source.

Reusable

17 agents, each declaring what it consumes, produces and costs. Any valid combination gets a verdict.

Grounded

All evidence comes from SerpApi plus the official RBI registry. The LLM never decides anything.

Everywhere

Web app, REST API, MCP server for Cursor and Claude, and scheduled watches with Telegram alerts.

Cost before compute

Agents declare their searches, so every workflow is priced and approved before it runs.

Receipts for every call

Engine, query, agent, source (paid, cache or replay) and duration, on every run.

Budgets and cache

Per-run and monthly caps. Repeated searches come from cache and cost nothing.

Trustworthy by design

Rules decide. The LLM only explains. RBI provides the ground truth.

Evidence status

CONTRADICTED

A high contradiction, or 2 or more

UNVERIFIED

One contradiction, a high gap, or open questions

CORROBORATED

2 or more independent supports

INSUFFICIENT

Fewer than 3 evidence items

30 rules return contradictions, gaps, signals and supports, each linked to its evidence.

Confidence, factor by factor

Explanations in English, Hindi or Tamil. Works with no LLM at all.

The stack in seven layers
Beyond verification · a platform

The same agents power research, grounded ads, alerts and AI tools.

Market Pulse

Trends momentum and top states, rising searches, the live price band, competitor advertisers and review pain points in one sourced brief.

Ad Studio

Google, Instagram, LinkedIn and WhatsApp ads. Every claim links to evidence; unsupported superlatives and below-market prices are flagged.

Watches

Re-run on a schedule, diff 12 metrics, alert on Telegram or email only when something changed.

MCP server

Six tools for Cursor and Claude: verify_claim, run_agent, run_workflow, market_pulse and more.

REST API

Start runs, stream SSE, fetch verdicts and PDF reports with hashed, revocable tokens.

Open-source LLMs

Ollama, LM Studio or llama.cpp. A 3B model is enough because rules decide.
